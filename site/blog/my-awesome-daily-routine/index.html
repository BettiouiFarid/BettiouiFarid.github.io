My awesome daily routine

Lifestyle

Waking up at dawn has persistently been a hurdle for me. Only infrequently have I succeeded to greet the dawn by getting up early, rather than being an all-nighter. Nonetheless, since adopting this practice, my early hours have significantly improved and are gratifying. Adhering to a regular daily regimen and habits enhances cognitive performance and perhaps fires up imaginative thinking.

Routine practices afford us some authority over our daily life, enabling us to take preemptive steps in managing our well-being. Allocating a slot for physical activities in our daily agendas aids in meeting the advised activity recommendations. Waking up is much like becoming more aware of our thoughts and preventing our past from shaping our future. Acknowledging the reasons for the derailment of our good intentioned plans, and the instigators that make us fall back on old habits that no longer fit our current scenario.
